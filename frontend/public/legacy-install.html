Add Vanni Ride to your home screen

Your Android version uses a browser shortcut for reliable compatibility. Vanni Ride will open safely in Chrome.

  1. Tap Prepare shortcut.
  2. Open Chrome's three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen.

Shortcut ready. Now open Chrome's three-dot menu and choose Add to Home screen.

When you use the new icon, it will open Vanni Ride in Chrome instead of the unsupported installed-app shell.
